# Pingato Personal Noncommercial Software License

The **Pingato Personal Noncommercial Software License (PNSL)** is a source-available software license for personal, noncommercial use by individuals.

It permits individuals to use, study, modify, and redistribute software for their own personal, noncommercial purposes while requiring redistributed software to remain under the same license.

## What PNSL Permits

PNSL permits a natural person, acting solely in their individual capacity, to:

- use the software for personal, noncommercial purposes;
- inspect, study, test, and experiment with the software;
- modify the software for permitted purposes; and
- redistribute the original software or permitted modifications under the same license.

## What PNSL Does Not Permit

PNSL does not grant permission for use by or on behalf of:

- employers or clients;
- businesses or corporations;
- nonprofit or charitable organizations;
- educational or research institutions;
- governments, government agencies, or public bodies; or
- other organizations or legal entities.

Commercial, professional, employment-related, organizational, institutional, and governmental use requires a separate license or permission from the software's licensor.

An individual's employment or affiliation does not by itself prevent personal use. The distinction is whether the individual is using the software solely in their personal capacity rather than for or on behalf of an organization or another person.

## Redistribution

Redistribution is permitted, including permitted modifications, but the software must remain subject to PNSL. It may not be relicensed or sublicensed under different terms.
